Methodology

The NORDR Approach

Direction Through Understanding.

Human Performance begins with understanding what the organisation is trying to achieve.

Start with the objective

Clarity begins with three questions.

  • What are we trying to achieve?
  • What needs to be true for that outcome to occur?
  • How is the Human Performance system expected to contribute?

Elements

Each element in relation

01
Objective
The organisational outcome Human Performance activity exists to support.
02
System
The people, services, processes and structures intended to contribute to that objective.
03
Human
What enables or constrains people expected to perform within that system.
04
Evidence
The information available to understand activity, experience and effect.
05
Decision
How evidence reaches the people who need it, and how insight becomes action.
06
Outcome
What happens as a result, and whether the intended effect can be determined.
07
Feedback
How learning from outcomes informs the next cycle of understanding and decision.

Look for relationships, not isolated metrics

Individual measures can be useful. Interpretation depends on context and relationship to the organisational objective.

NORDR does not treat isolated indicators as sufficient explanations of Human Performance. The aim is clearer understanding of how elements connect — not claims of prediction or causation.

Evidence before assumption

NORDR seeks to distinguish:

  • what is known
  • what is observed
  • what is reported
  • what is inferred
  • what still needs to be investigated
