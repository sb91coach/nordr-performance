People. Systems. Outcomes.

What are you trying to achieve?

Human Performance should begin with the outcome the organisation is trying to achieve.

Organisations may already have people, services, processes, information and support in place. The key question is how those components collectively contribute to the outcome that matters.

NORDR examines the relationship between these elements rather than treating Human Performance as a collection of isolated interventions.

Understand before you change

Before deciding what needs changing, understand the system.

NORDR examines:

  • what the organisation is trying to achieve
  • how the current system is intended to support that objective
  • what enables or constrains people within that system
  • what evidence is available
  • how evidence informs decisions
  • what happens as a result
  • how learning feeds back into the system
